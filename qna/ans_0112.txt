ON VIOLATION FAIL UPDATE instructs the pipeline to immediately stop processing and fail the transaction when a data record violates the specified expectation constraint. Official documentation details DLT constraints under Manage data quality with Delta Live Tables on Databricks Docs.

B is incorrect: 
ON VIOLATION DROP ROW discards the violating record silently without failing the pipeline.

C is incorrect: 
ON VIOLATION WARN records the violation in the metrics log and allows the record to pass through, without halting execution.

D is incorrect:
